abstract: >-
  We introduce ReGraph3D, a lightweight framework for language-guided 3D indoor scene rearrangement. We further introduce BSSG-FRONT, extending SSR-3DFRONT with relational scene graphs for 14,992 indoor scenes. ReGraph3D achieves over 90% accuracy for directional spatial relations and performs pose generation with only 1.44M parameters in approximately 0.04 seconds per scene.
